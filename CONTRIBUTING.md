# Contributing

FiliArrochada projects are small and maintained by one person, so contributions are welcome but best
started with a conversation:

- **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue in the project's repository, with steps to reproduce for a bug.
- **Pull requests:** for anything beyond a small fix, open an issue first so we can agree on the approach.
- **Security issues:** don't open a public issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

By contributing you agree that your work is released under the project's licence.
